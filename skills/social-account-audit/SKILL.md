# Social Account Audit

Read-only review of a creator or brand's social accounts, turned into decisions. By The Ask Why Company.

## Where the data is
- Instagram + Facebook: Meta Business Suite → Insights (Overview, Content, Audience); Instagram's "most active times" is often only in the mobile apps (Instagram / Edits).
- TikTok: TikTok Studio → Analytics (Overview, Content, Viewers, Followers).
- YouTube: YouTube Studio → Analytics.
Only read. Never post, follow, like, message or change settings during an audit.

## Collect per account (last 28–30 days, plus lifetime audience)
- Views, viewers/reach, **share from non-followers**, follows vs unfollows (net change), profile visits.
- Posting frequency and date of the last post.
- Top content: views, average watch time, shares/sends, saves, comments — and what kind of content it was.
- Audience: gender, age bands, top countries/cities.
- Traffic sources (TikTok: For You vs Search vs profile).
- Most active days and hours (with time zone).

## Read the signals
- Net follower loss + no recent posts = dormancy, not bad content. Fix: post again consistently.
- Mostly non-follower reach = every hook must work cold.
- Search-heavy traffic with zero recommendation traffic = the platform isn't pushing the account (usually inactivity); put searchable keywords in captions and on-screen text.
- High shares/sends on a post type = that's the format to repeat.
- Different audiences per platform (age, country) = same video, different caption/angle per platform.
- International audience = check app/store availability and offer metric/imperial units.

## Output
A one-page summary: per-account table, 5–8 implications, recommended post times per platform, and the formats to double down on. Save it where the team keeps planning docs.
